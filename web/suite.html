      Links

      When the chalet last heard each hub directly on LoRa, and on ESP-NOW (with the relays passed). Backbone = the hub also sends over ESP-NOW right now (Auto fallback or ESP-NOW mode). Transport and channel are set in Settings → Network.

      Range test — walk with the chalet box

      One hub stays at its hole; its turn cycles through every LoRa speed (SF9 → SF5, one per second) while you carry the chalet box around. Every result is logged with the box's GPS position and its distance to the hub. Trips and alerts keep working; only that hub's sonar is thin while a speed fails.

      Runs on the chalet

      Download a run (CSV: time, GPS, distance, speed, received, RSSI, SNR, and how the hub heard the chalet) and send it over: the speed limits get measured instead of estimated. Stops by itself after 2 h, when storage runs low, or when the hub is gone for 5 min.

      Radio test modes

      Adaptive speed per hub (SF9 → SF5 on signal margin; off = SF9 for a range test)

      Hubs

      Per hub and per LoRa mode: packets received / slots scheduled at the chalet, RSSI/SNR at the chalet. Beacon columns: the chalet beacon as heard by the hub, beacons lost in the last 64, timing error. Sensitivity (theory, not measured): SF9/500 −123.5, SF8/500 −121, SF7/500 −118.5 dBm.

      Measurements

      Wiring check

      Asks a hole to test itself: sensor pin, arming button, battery, sonar shield (SPI, VDRV, a test ping) and radio. The result comes back over the mesh: a hub answers in a few seconds, a sleeping tip-up at its next heartbeat (up to 1 min).

      Map storage

      Map tiles come from the internet through the phone; every tile seen stays on this phone, and Save area on the Holes tab keeps the tiles of an area on the chalet for any phone on the ice. Depth contours (optional, where CHS has data): a pack made on the PC with tools/lake_prep.py.

      LoRa & relays

      Activity heard by the chalet per channel (other LoRa users, %)
      ESP-NOW relays (rebroadcast between hubs and the chalet, max 2 in a row)

      Hubs follow these settings through the beacon: nothing to reprogram on the ice. A channel change is announced 6 beacons ahead.

      Test & simulation

      Fake sonar keeps a tip-up awake (battery). Fake fish go through the real alert path (buzzer, phone) and are marked SIM everywhere. Test holes on the hubs are switched from the Sonar tab and never survive a reboot. Turn the rest off after testing.

      About

      Hubs take the network settings (LoRa channel, relays) from the chalet; their other settings are set on each hub. Hub ↔ chalet link: ESP-NOW while a hub hears the chalet on it, LoRa otherwise, by itself.
