Processing

    Simulated pings modelled on a 200 kHz, 14° transducer over 18.5 ft of water: a jigged lure at 15 ft, weeds on the bottom, a perch, a walleye that comes in and strikes, and a fish passing through. Real recordings will look different. Use this to tune the display, not to trust the numbers.
